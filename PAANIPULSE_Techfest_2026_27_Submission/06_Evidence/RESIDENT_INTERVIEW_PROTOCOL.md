# PAANIPULSE — Resident Interview Protocol & Validation Framework
## Techfest 2026–27 Campus to Cloud Hackathon sponsored by AWS
*Prepared for Student Field Research and Real-World Validation*

---

### 1. Ethical Voluntary Consent Statement
> *“Hello! I am a student conducting academic research for the Techfest 2026–27 Hackathon on urban water accessibility in Indian neighbourhoods. 
> We are studying how residents verify daily tap water supply timing and disruptions. 
> Your participation is completely voluntary. We will NEVER ask for your name, phone number, flat number, or house address. 
> Only your general neighbourhood/ward name and daily water experience will be noted. 
> May I ask you 5 brief questions? It takes under 3 minutes.”*

---

### 2. Five Concise Resident Interview Questions

#### Question 1: Daily Supply Routine
*“At what approximate time does municipal water supply typically arrive in your locality, and for roughly how many hours does it flow?”*
- Options to record: Morning (6–10 AM) / Afternoon (10 AM–4 PM) / Evening (4–9 PM) / Night (9 PM–6 AM) / Unpredictable

#### Question 2: The Information Dilemma
*“When the tap does NOT flow at the scheduled time, how do you usually find out what happened?”*
- Options to record: WhatsApp society chat / Calling the building watchman / Calling neighbours / Waiting passively / Calling municipal ward helpline

#### Question 3: Frequency of False Alarms
*“In the past month, has there been an instance where you thought there was a municipal water cut, but it turned out to be an internal building issue (such as a tripped motor switch or empty society tank)?”*
- Options to record: Yes, frequently / Yes, once or twice / No / Not sure

#### Question 4: Low-Pressure Impact
*“How often does water arrive with low pressure, making it difficult to fill upper-floor tanks or overhead storages?”*
- Options to record: Daily / 2–3 times a week / Rarely / Never

#### Question 5: Civic Tool Receptivity
*“If a free mobile web tool showed you live, verified water observations from nearby neighbours and warned you before the tap runs dry, would you use it or contribute a quick 10-second update?”*
- Options to record: Highly Likely / Likely / Neutral / Unlikely
