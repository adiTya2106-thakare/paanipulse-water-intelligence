5. PROBLEM STATEMENT (SHORT - 60 WORDS):
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Over 350 million urban Indian residents face intermittent municipal water rationing (1 to 3 hours daily). When water is delayed or pressure drops, residents panic: is it a building motor pump failure or a municipal ward-wide outage? Today, citizens rely on informal, frantic WhatsApp messages and unverified rumors, leading to booster pump burnouts and unnecessary private water tanker expenses.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
6. PROPOSED SOLUTION (SHORT - 75 WORDS):
----------------------------------------------------------------------
PAANIPULSE is a lightweight, mobile-first civic water intelligence network that organizes voluntary resident observations into transparent, explainable neighbourhood signals. In under 15 seconds, citizens log water condition and pressure. PAANIPULSE compares submissions against a diurnal Neighbourhood Water Fingerprint (Morning, Midday, Evening, Night), calculates an objective 100-point Community Signal Confidence score, and actively flags early disruption anomalies—without requiring expensive, fragile IoT pipe hardware.

----------------------------------------------------------------------

8. BHARAT IMPACT (EVALUATION WEIGHT: 30%):
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Empowers Indian households, apartment societies (RWAs), and chawl committees to plan domestic chores responsibly, avoid dry pump motor burnout, and prevent private tanker price gouging. Provides downloadable, evidence-based audit report cards that citizens can present to local municipal corporators (MCGM, PMC, BWSSB, DJB) to highlight chronic low-pressure distribution zones.

----------------------------------------------------------------------
9. INNOVATION & DIFFERENTIATORS (EVALUATION WEIGHT: 25%):
----------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Diurnal Neighbourhood Water Fingerprint: Maps historical baseline by diurnal time intervals with honest developing baseline states (<12 reports).
2. Transparent Community Confidence Meter: 100-point composite formula factoring reporter diversity, recency decay, and neighbour agreement.
3. Explainable Disruption Early-Warning: Statistically detects >=50% drops below baseline without hallucinating official outages.
4. Privacy-by-Design: Centroid-level ward mapping with zero private address storage.
