# PAANIPULSE — Demo Video Recording Checklist & Visual Guide
## Techfest 2026–27 Campus to Cloud Hackathon sponsored by AWS

### Video Production Rules (From Official Hackathon Guidelines)
- **Duration:** Strictly under 3:00 minutes (Ideal: 2:45 to 2:55).
- **Format:** MP4 format, 1080p (1920x1080) landscape.
- **Audio:** Clear voiceover with minimal background noise.
- **File Name:** `DEMO_VIDEO.mp4` (placed in `03_Live_Demo/` folder).

---

### Free Tools for Quick Recording & Editing
1. **OBS Studio (Recommended):** Free, open source screen recorder. Set resolution to 1920x1080, 60fps.
2. **Clipchamp / CapCut / DaVinci Resolve:** Trim pauses and ensure the video stops at 2:50.
3. **Audacity:** Remove background mic hiss if recording in a dorm or noisy room.
